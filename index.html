FAQs

Questions, answered

Is online consultation as effective as visiting in person?

Yes. There is no clinic to visit — every assessment, plan and check-in happens over WhatsApp message, whether you’re in Lahore or anywhere else in the world. Nothing about the process is lost by being online.

I have PCOS and diabetes together — can one plan handle both?

Yes. Your assessment covers all existing conditions, so your plan is built to manage overlapping concerns like insulin resistance, hormones and blood sugar together — not as separate diets.

How soon will I see results?

Most clients notice changes in energy and digestion within the first 2 weeks, with measurable weight and symptom changes by week 4–6, depending on your starting point and consistency.

What do the programs cost?

Pricing depends on your program and duration. Message Noor on WhatsApp for a free assessment and a package recommendation tailored to your goals.

Will I have to give up the foods I love?

No. Wherever you’re based — Pakistan, the Gulf, the UK, North America or anywhere else — your plan is built around your own cuisine and everyday routine, portioned and paired correctly, not eliminated.
